# InkOS trade partnerships

InkOS is building cloud print management software for print shops. We welcome wholesale print, signage, finishing and fabrication partners who want buying shops to be able to send them work through InkOS.

## How we approach an integration

We start with the ordering system your company uses today. If you publish an API, its documentation can guide the integration. If you use a trade portal, a file exchange or another workflow, describe how a buying shop places an order and receives updates.

Together we can identify a first workflow, such as requesting a price for a postcard run, sending approved artwork for CNC cutting or returning shipment tracking to the buying shop. Your team defines its production capabilities, trade pricing and account terms. The trade printer invoices the buying shop and collects payment directly.

## Introduce your company

Tell us what your company produces, how you receive trade orders today, and which part of the workflow you would like to connect first. You can include a link to integration documentation that your company already makes public. The issue gives both teams a place to ask questions and agree on the scope of a potential integration.

GitHub issues are public. Keep customer information, artwork, account-specific pricing, credentials and private documentation out of your submission. Your GitHub account is sufficient for initial contact.
